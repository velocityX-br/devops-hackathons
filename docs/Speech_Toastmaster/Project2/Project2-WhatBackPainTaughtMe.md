## Title: The Back Pain Blessing: My Body’s Red Light

### Opening (1-2 Minutes)

- Greeting: "Mr./Madam" Toastmaster, fellow members, and guests..
- Hook: Question driven hook - "Raise your hand if you have ever experienced a little bit of lower back pain... [Pause, wait hands go up]... Now, keep your hand up if that pain have ever been so intense, so moderate, or so high that it completely froze you in your tracks [Pause, look around the room]"
- The Metaphor: Lower back pain could be a red light to overall health
- Transition: That exact painful experience led me realize that I need to make a stop.



### Briefing the story (~2 Minutes)

Last weekend, I woke up, tried to get out of bed—and froze. A sharp pain shot through my lower back. For a few seconds, I just sat there, wondering how someone who sits in meetings all week could suddenly feel like he had run a marathon he never signed up for.

I had felt mild back discomfort before. It usually came, stayed a little while, and left. That weekend was different. 

So this morning, I want to share you what went wrong and how I get inspired from it.

#### Point1 - It wasn't a sudden suprise - my body had been sending me warning signs all along.

1. Warning signs were everywhere, stand up stiff, lower back ache after flights
2. Flighting oil light might not cause immediate engine failure, but consistently neglecting it will undoubtly lead to serious problems down the road.
3. Ignoring the body signal, no matter how small they seems, can have significant short term and long term consequences on your health and overall quality of life.



#### Point2 - I tried to buy my way out of the problem.**

1. Opened a shopping app and ordered and experienced Standing desk. Lumbar cushion, ergonomic chair in the following weeks
2. It is proven that buying things to fix a personal issue or habit doesn't work.
3. Hint to make a change



#### Point3 - Biological regulations rather than forced rest

1. Biological regulation is an active, internal process of balance, whereas forced rest is a passive, external imposition of stop. The body heals more effectively through biological regulation rather than forced rest,
2. Regular relaxtion plus proper exercise finally mitigated my lower back pain
3. Relexation is not only helpful for physical health but also Mental & Emotional Well-Being



### Conclusion (1-2 mins)

1. Don't neglect silent sign
2. Buy things to fix a personal habit doesn't work
3. Scientific Relaxation and know-how Stop is a key success to life
